- Position the franchise around the operating model, support, technology and economics without promising income, profit, order volume or investment recovery.
- Build evidence-backed franchise offer, onboarding and recruitment messaging.
- Develop customer acquisition and local-market playbooks for franchisees.
- Define central-brand/local-area SEO and conversion architecture.
- Create transparent unit-economics communication using validated assumptions.
- Establish lead → qualification → discovery → application → onboarding funnel measurement.

Build a proof-led franchise proposition and recruitment funnel around the actual operating system, service-area model and support structure. Keep financial claims configurable and evidence-gated.

## Boundary
